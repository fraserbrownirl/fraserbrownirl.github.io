Your Telegram channels, in one private group

Nov 20, 2026

Telegram is a good place to follow one channel and a bad place to follow twenty. There is no timeline. The post you wanted is under three you didn't, and the standard fix is to log a user account into a server and let it read your chats.

I don't want a bot holding my account. So this is only a bot. You make a private group, turn topics on, and add it. It opens three topics and fills them.

Newest is the channels you added, as the posts go up. Popular comes back every few hours with the ones people reacted to, scored against how recent they are, and scaled so a huge channel doesn't win by being huge. For You is channels that show up next to yours among people with a similar list. Tap the card, it's added. The product is the group. There is no website next to it.

A post doesn't move unless two people already said yes.

The channel owner adds the bot as an admin and ticks no permissions. The bot never writes in the channel. Remove it and the channel leaves the network. That's the owner's yes.

Yours is a forward. Send the bot any post from the channel. It calls getChatMember and asks Telegram what you are in that chat. Creator, administrator, or member, and the channel can join the feed. Anything else, and it can't. This is not a way to read something you weren't let into.

The other designs are the ones people actually ship. A mirror that logs in as you, which can see what you can see and also has your keys. Or a public repost account, which puts the channel in front of people who never joined. I wanted the dull one. A pipe for access you already have. If the channel turned content protection on, Telegram refuses the forward, and the bot does not copy the message out the side. They already said it stays.

It runs. It isn't finished. No mini app, no page of stats for the channel owner, and For You is not a model reading the posts and deciding you have taste. It looks at whose subscriptions overlap, and which posts are picking up reactions.

The shape, if you want to feel it: send the bot /start, make a group, turn Topics on, add the bot with permission to manage topics, then forward a post from a channel you're in. The owner has to have added the bot too, or it won't take. /list is what you're getting. /pause is how you get the afternoon back.
