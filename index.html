A personal Telegram feed

I built a personal Telegram feed using Cursor with Grok 4.7. It's for someone who already reads a pile of channels and wants them in one private group: Newest as posts arrive, Popular scored from reactions and how recent a post is, and For You from people who read a similar set. The bet is that a feed can be personal without a bot logging in as you.

What's new is a feed that only carries a post you were already allowed to see. A channel joins when its owner adds the bot as an admin, and a reader only gets it after Telegram confirms they are already in that channel. There is no user session behind it. If a channel has content protection on, the post is skipped instead of copied out. The usual mirror either logs in as you and pulls every chat, or republishes a channel to people who were never members. This one does neither. A pipe for access you already have.

The check itself is getChatMember. Before a channel joins the feed, the bot asks Telegram for your status in that chat and continues only if the status is creator, administrator, or member.

The aim is a Telegram you can actually keep up with, where the feed is yours, the channel still belongs to the person who runs it, and a post stays where it is when the channel asked it not to leave.
