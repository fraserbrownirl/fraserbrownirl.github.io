parrot-token is the contracts for the parrot World App mini-app: 21 million PARROT minted once, split between a join curve and a referral tranche.

Claims use Path B: parrot already verifies World ID 4.0 Orb proofs off-chain. The backend signs an EIP-712 authorization with rank and referrer; ParrotDistributor on-chain checks the signature, dedupes nullifiers, pays the curve amount and referral reward. The claimSigner is trusted for human verification; amounts and caps are enforced in Solidity.

Referral rewards pay when the referee claims, not at signup. Self-referral is blocked and each referrer has a cap. After the claim window, undistributed tokens can sweep to treasury.

Foundry project with submodules — not in the app monorepo on purpose.
