Dental Hygiene Offers

Nov 23, 2026

Dental Hygiene Offers is a Supabase-backed app for dentists to list subscription-style hygiene offers and see subscribers and revenue on a dashboard.

The public side invites patients to find exclusive hygiene offers in their city. The dentist side creates offers with price and capacity, tracks active offers, and sees revenue from current subscribers.

It is a Lovable-hosted React stack. Production domain and polish are still open; the data model and dentist dashboard are the working center.
