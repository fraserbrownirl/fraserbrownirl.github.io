X to Telegram Mirror

Nov 23, 2026

This repo is the hosted mirror half of T-Rex's design experiment: one person's Bearer token, one X account, posts copied into a Telegram channel on a schedule.

monitor.py runs under Actions cron. It reads state/last_seen.json, fetches new tweets from the X API v2 for a configured username, and sends formatted HTML messages to Telegram. Replies, reposts, and quote posts stay out of scope — originals only after setup.

It is not a product strangers run; it is infrastructure I deploy with secrets in the repo's Actions settings. T-Rex is the reader-facing bot; this repo is the firehose mirror for a single handle.
