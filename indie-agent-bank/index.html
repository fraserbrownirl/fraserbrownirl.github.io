Indie Agent Bank

Nov 23, 2026

Indie Agent Bank is a treasury console for humans who want agents to spend with limits. The owner completes KYB in Dakota's sandbox, gets a business wallet and on-ramp, and mints agent API keys scoped by budget and payee allowlists.

An agent calls pay with amount, payee, and reason. Policy checks caps, monthly budget, and auto-approve thresholds. Approved spends sign USDC from the treasury wallet to fund Dakota off-ramps. Every decision lands in an audit ledger.

The live sandbox app is at indie-agent-bank.vercel.app. Signing keys in Postgres are a demo tradeoff; production would keep keys in KMS. For a walkthrough I usually send people to a demo clip on X rather than this page — this article is the static postcard.
